Business Pro Local Service Agreement

By selecting a Business Pro Local website package, you authorize Business Pro Local to design and set up a website for your business. On monthly plans, Business Pro Local also hosts, maintains, and supports your website.

Your selected package determines your one-time setup fee and, on monthly plans, your monthly website service fee. Major redesigns, custom systems, or services outside your selected package may require a separate quote.

Monthly service includes website hosting, routine maintenance, technical support, security assistance, backups, and reasonable minor website updates.

ProGo: the one-time fee covers a 5-page website, a free logo design, Google-ready SEO, and Google Business Profile setup. There is no monthly fee. Before handoff, ProGo includes 4 rounds of changes to your website; a round is one list of changes sent to us together. Each additional round of changes is $40. We put your website online using hosting and a web address set up in your own name, then hand the finished website files over to you. After handoff, you are responsible for your own hosting and web address renewals, and changes are quoted separately. ProGo websites do not include online booking, owner portals, online payments, or monthly support.

If you choose Business Pro Local Advertising (monthly plans only), you authorize your business to be advertised on participating non-competing Business Pro Local websites according to the advertising rate and minimum associated with your selected package.

Business Pro Local does not guarantee website traffic, advertising clicks, leads, customers, or sales.

Website development begins after this agreement is accepted and payment requirements are completed. Once you approve your website preview, please allow 5 to 7 business days for your website to go live.

Your website is built on the Business Pro design and customized with your logo, colors, photos, hours, services and contact details. Custom features or design changes beyond these can be quoted separately as an add-on.

On monthly plans, your first month of monthly service is free. After that, monthly service is billed in advance. You may cancel at any time by email or text. If you cancel partway through a paid month, you will receive a prorated refund for the unused days of that month.

The setup fee is non-refundable once website development has begun.

If a monthly payment is not received, Business Pro Local may pause or turn off your website until your account is brought up to date.

You keep ownership of your logo, photos, and business content. The Business Pro design, platform, and software remain the property of Business Pro Local. ProGo customers own their finished website files after handoff.

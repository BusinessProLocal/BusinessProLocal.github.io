My Story

Business Pro started from the basement. I've spent most of my life working with my hands. I started as a laborer on construction sites and worked my way up to running heavy machinery. When I moved to a roof truss company, I started at the bottom again and worked my way back up to running the loader and driving truck. In 2012, a head-on collision changed everything. What followed were some of the darkest years of my life. With the help of my doctors and modern medicine, I found my way back.

For years, the dream of building my own company sat on a shelf. With only some high school, no business degree and no business training, I had no idea where to begin. The only way I could build a business was from home. I knew computers well enough to build my own from the parts up, but knowing computers isn't the same as knowing how to start a business. Still, I figured it out, one step at a time. I built Business Pro the same way I built my life: from the ground up. I know what it takes to build something from nothing, and that's who Business Pro is for: the people who unlock the doors every morning and keep their town running.

I'm a Maine guy who knows hard work and answers my own phone. When you join, you're not an account number. You're a neighbor. I built mine from the ground up, and now I want to help you do the same. Let's build yours together. Start small, grow big, and join the Business Pro family.

Tom, Founder
